Past results

Every month, ₹10,000 goes into two plans on the first trading day of the month. The monthly DCA plan invests it at once. The volatility-managed plan sets how much of its money is in the index from the previous month's realized variance, and keeps the rest in cash, which earns nothing. Both plans use the same money and the same prices.

Settings and your own data

A CSV needs a date column and a close column. Dates may be 27-Oct-2025, 27-10-2025 or 2025-10-27, in any order. Your file is used on the live chart and the statistics tab.

How this differs from the paper
  • Method. This is a practical monthly-contribution adaptation, not an exact replication of Moreira and Muir (2017). The paper scales the excess return of a factor with a constant c matched to the unmanaged volatility.
  • Contributions. Money is added every month, so the volatility plan starts with little in the index. The paper has no monthly contributions.
  • Target. The target uses last month's realized variance (sum of squared daily returns), inverted and divided by the average of all earlier months' inverse variance, capped at 100%. The paper scales with its own full-sample constant, which uses hindsight.
  • Cash. Idle cash earns 0%. No risk-free rate is used, so the Sharpe ratio has a risk-free rate of 0.
  • Prices. Price indices without dividends, for both plans.
  • Sample. The sample is about 72 monthly observations against the paper's 1,065 (2020 to 2025 for the bundled data). Treat the result as one short sample.

Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty closes: official NSE files supplied by the owner (2020 to 2025). Price indices without dividends. Method: Moreira and Muir, Volatility-Managed Portfolios, Journal of Finance (2017), adapted to monthly contributions. Historical back-test, not investment advice.
