Volatility-managed portfolios · NSE

Hold less when the market is shaking. Measure whether it helps.

Volatility Lab back-tests a simple rule on real Nifty 50 and Bank Nifty prices, 2019 to 2026: put in Rs 10,000 every month, or hold more or less of a Rs 10 lakh pool in the index depending on how rough the last 20 trading days were. Based on Moreira and Muir, Volatility-Managed Portfolios (SSRN 2659431).

Method

How the rule works

The maths follows the paper. You can reproduce any number on the page from the same CSV.

Measure the swings

Average the squared daily moves of the last 20 trading days.

v = mean of r squared over 20 days

Set the share of the pool

Hold less when recent swings are larger than usual, more when they are calmer, never more than the cap you choose.

share = typical variance / v, between your least and most

Compare honestly

Both plans start with the same Rs 10,00,000. Compare final value, biggest fall from a high, and return per unit of risk, after trading costs.

value = pool x (1 + share x daily move) - costs

Monthly SIP

Rs 10,000 goes into the index on the first trading day of each month. The rest of the pool stays in cash.

Volatility-based

The share of the pool held in the index is reset each month from the last 20 days of volatility. The rest stays in cash.
