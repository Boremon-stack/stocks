# Owner method study log, 2026-10-27

Method: monthly dollar-cost averaging (DCA) against a volatility-managed plan (VM) on the same monthly contributions. Each plan receives Rs 10,000 on the first trading day of each month (monthly 10,000, maximum exposure 100%, cost 0 bp). DCA buys the full amount at once. VM sets its target share of the index from last month's realized variance, scaled by the mean of earlier scaled values and capped at the maximum exposure, and rebalances to that share on each contribution day. Idle cash earns 0%. Data are daily closes from the NSE official files in public/data, price indices without dividends, 2020-01-01 to 2025-12-31.

## Limits

72 monthly observations for Nifty 50 and 72 monthly observations for Bank Nifty, from 2020-01-01 to 2025-12-31, so every statistic rests on one short sample. Price indices exclude dividends, so index returns are understated against total return. Cash earns 0%. The first two months have target 0 by construction, because no prior month variance exists yet. The method is a practical adaptation of Moreira and Muir (2017), not an exact replication of that paper. No taxes or slippage beyond the cost input (0 bp here). Results are a historical back-test, not investment advice.
