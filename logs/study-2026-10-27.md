# Past results study log, 2026-10-27

Data are daily closes of ^NSEI and ^NSEBANK from Yahoo Finance, 2019-01-02 to 2026-10-27, price indices without dividends. Both plans start from a pool of Rs 10,00,000, and the monthly SIP of Rs 10,000 is drawn from that same pool. The volatility plan sets its share of the pool at each monthly reset from the 20-day realized variance, capped at 100% of the pool with no leverage and 0% as the floor, and pays a cost of 0.10% per unit of exposure change. Two calibrations are reported. The paper calibration sets one constant c over the whole period (the paper's normalisation, which uses hindsight). The running-average calibration uses the expanding average of past variance as the reference instead. Idle cash earns 0% and the risk-free rate is 0%. The 100% cap follows the paper's No Leverage variant (Table IV, p.45: alpha 2.12% against 4.86% unconstrained). Raw prices are not committed except the bundled public/data CSVs.

## Independent check

The tests in test/backtest.test.mjs and test/compare_closed_form.test.mjs compare the JS engine with independent pandas, scipy and statsmodels implementations (test/reference_compare.py and test/reference_study.py), field by field. Running node --test test/backtest.test.mjs test/compare_closed_form.test.mjs reports 61 passing and 0 failing tests; the raw output is in logs/test-2026-10-27.txt. Regenerate the fixtures with python test/reference_compare.py and python test/reference_study.py. Python package versions (pandas, numpy, scipy, statsmodels): 3.0.5 2.4.6 1.17.1 0.15.0.

## Limits

One sample period (2019-01-02 to 2026-10-27): 94 monthly observations for Nifty 50 and 94 for Bank Nifty, against the paper's 1,065, so the paper statistics have low power. The constant c is set over the whole period with hindsight, as in the paper. The cap is 100% of the pool, which is the paper's No Leverage variant. Idle cash and the risk-free rate are 0%. Price indices exclude dividends, so index returns are understated against total return. The SIP is funded from the same pool, and the paper statistics are computed at zero cost with costs shown separately. One cost assumption (0.10% per unit of exposure change) drives the cost rows, with the alpha-after-cost rows at 1, 10 and 14 bp. No taxes. Results depend on the period and these assumptions and are not investment advice.
